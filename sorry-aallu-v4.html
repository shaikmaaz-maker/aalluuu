My letter to you

My dear, my ziddi Aallu,

You did it. You walked all the way here, and that already tells me how patient and kind your heart is, even when I did not deserve it.

I am sorry. Not the quick kind of sorry that only wants an uncomfortable moment to end, but the real kind, the one that sits quietly with what I did and understands how it made you feel.

I know I hurt you. I will not explain it away or hide behind reasons. You trusted me with your feelings, and I should have held them more gently. That is on me, and only me.

You are stubborn, and I love that about you. It is the same strength that makes you stand up for yourself and for the people you love. I never want to be the reason that strength has to guard your heart against me.

When I think of you, I think of the way you laugh when you believe nobody is watching, and the way you turn an ordinary day into something worth remembering. Life is quieter and colder without that, and I miss it when we are apart.

I promise to listen before I answer, to be patient when you need time, and to say sorry sooner instead of letting silence grow between us. I will show it in what I do, not only in what I write.

You do not have to forgive me today. Take all the time you need. I will be right here, in good days and hard ones, not going anywhere.

I love you, Aallu. Truly, and always.

Yours, always 🤍
